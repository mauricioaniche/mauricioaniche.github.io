## Short bio

### English

Maurício Aniche is the CTO of Alun, the largest technology, business, and AI ecosystem in Latin America. His career combines industry leadership with academic research, bringing together deep expertise in software engineering, software quality, and engineering leadership.

Before joining Alun, Maurício was a Staff Engineer at Uber, led the Testing Enablement team at Adyen, and served as an Assistant Professor of Software Engineering at Delft University of Technology in the Netherlands from 2019 to 2024. His research focused on software quality, covering topics such as testing, maintainability, and engineering practices.

His contributions to education earned him the Computer Science Teacher of the Year 2021 award and the prestigious TU Delft Education Fellowship, awarded to innovative and impactful educators. Maurício is also the author of Effective Software Testing: A Developer’s Guide (2022) and Simple Object-Oriented Design: Create Clean, Maintainable Applications (2024), both published by Manning.

### Versão em português (Brasil)

Maurício Aniche é CTO da Alun, o maior ecossistema de tecnologia, negócios e IA da América Latina. Sua trajetória combina liderança na indústria e pesquisa acadêmica, reunindo experiência profunda em engenharia de software, qualidade de software, e liderança de times.

Antes de ingressar na Alun, Maurício atuou como Staff Engineer na Uber, liderou a equipe de Testing Enablement da Adyen e foi Professor Assistente de Engenharia de Software na Delft University of Technology, na Holanda, entre 2019 e 2024. Sua pesquisa concentrou-se em diferentes aspectos da qualidade de software, incluindo testes, manutenibilidade e práticas de engenharia.

Seu trabalho em educação lhe rendeu o prêmio Computer Science Teacher of the Year 2021 e o prestigiado TU Delft Education Fellowship, concedido a docentes que se destacam pela inovação e impacto em ensino. Maurício também é autor dos livros Effective Software Testing: A Developer's Guide (2022) e Simple Object-Oriented Design: Create Clean, Maintainable Applications (2024), ambos publicados pela Manning.
