Maurício Aniche, CTO of Alura,ex-{Uber, Adyen, Locaweb, TU Delft}, and published author.

Background. I got my PhD (2016) and MSc (2012) degrees in Computer Science at University of São Paulo, Brazil. I worked as a software developer for Locaweb, Emphasys, and VeriFone. I was a founding engineer at Alura, the largest educational platform for software engineers in Brazil.

Book author. I am the author of Effective Software Testing: A Developer's Guide (2022), and Simple Object-Oriented Design (2024), both published by Manning. I have also authored books on Test-Driven Development and on the SOLID principles, in Brazilian Portuguese.

Awards. I was among the top 20 active early-stage career software engineering researchers, as shown by a 2021 bibliometrics paper. I was also elected the 2021 TU Delft Teacher of the Year in Computer Science.
