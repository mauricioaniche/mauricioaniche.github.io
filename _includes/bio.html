Maurício Aniche, hands-on tech lead at Adyen,former assistant professor in software engineering at TU Delft, and published author.

Adyen. I lead the Testing Enablement team at Adyen. Our team focuses on making sure our engineers have the right tools at hand to ensure the quality of their software systems.

TU Delft. I used to be an assistant professor in Software Engineering. My research focused on helping developers to effectively maintain and evolve their software systems. I also taught Software Testing and Quality (CS1110) to computer science bachelor students for many years.

Background. I got my PhD (2016) and MSc (2012) degrees in Computer Science at University of São Paulo, Brazil. I worked as a software developer for Locaweb, Emphasys, and VeriFone. I was a founding engineer at Alura, the largest educational platform for software engineers in Brazil.

Book author. I am the author of Effective Software Testing: A Developer's Guide (2022), and Simple Object-Oriented Design (2024), both published by Manning. I have also authored books on Test-Driven Development and on the SOLID principles, in Brazilian Portuguese.

Awards. I was elected the 2021 TU Delft Teacher of the Year in Computer Science. In a 2021 bibliometrics paper, I was among the top 20 active early-stage career software engineering researchers.
