Hi, I'm Maurício Aniche, a Software Engineering Researcher at TU Delft.

Maurício holds a PhD from University of São Paulo. He likes to have one foot in research and one foot in industry. During his PhD, Maurício co-founded Alura, the most popular e-learning platform for software engineers in Brazil. Maurício has authored two books (in portuguese) about pragmatic testing (TDD in Real World) and object-oriented design (OOP and SOLID principles for Ninjas) which have sold more than 7k copies.

His research interests are empirical software engineering, more specifically in software testing, software architecture, software maintenance, and software security.
